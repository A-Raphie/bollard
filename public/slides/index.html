The Vulnerability
02 / 06

Voice to Robot Execution is Catastrophic Without Physical Guardrails

VLA (Vision-Language-Action) models and LLMs hallucinate coordinates, misunderstand spoken homophones, and ignore physical envelope limits when executing dual-arm manipulation.

FAILURE MODE 01
Acoustic Misparsing
In noisy industrial environments, speech engines drop negatives, confuse homophones ("right" vs "light"), and mangle units ("10 cm" vs "10 meters"), leading directly to high-velocity collisions.
FAILURE MODE 02
Physics & Envelope Blindness
LLMs lack real-time kinematic spatial awareness. They happily issue commands exceeding dual-arm reach, collision paths between left and right end-effectors, or over-payload grasp limits.
FAILURE MODE 03
Zero Audit Trail
When an industrial arm crushes hardware, post-incident investigations find ambiguous logs. There is no verifiable proof of what audio was transcribed, what rule passed, or what actuator moved.

Cryptographic Accountability
05 / 06

Cryptographic Receipts: Tamper-Evident Audit Ledger

Replacing opaque robot log files with mathematical proof of policy compliance.

01 • CANONICAL PAYLOAD
Deterministic Serialization
Every command record serializes:
{ id, timestamp, transcript, arm: "BOTH", rule: "POLICY_ENVELOPE", verdict: "ALLOWED", prevHash }
02 • HASH LINKING
SHA-256 Merkle Chain
Each receipt embeds the cryptographic hash of the preceding receipt. Any modification to past voice commands or verdicts immediately breaks the chain validation.
03 • INSTANT VERIFICATION
Client-Side Proof
Operators and regulatory inspectors can verify the entire sequence with zero reliance on cloud trust. 100% verifiable offline and on-premise.
